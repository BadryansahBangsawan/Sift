# Third-party notices

Sift's Apple Intelligence tools are a port of [RemoveMacAI](https://github.com/omlahore/RemoveMacAI)
by Om Lahore. Its catalog of Apple Intelligence features and model sets, the
configuration profile that blocks their downloads, the settings journal and the
undo behaviour follow that project's `Catalog.swift`, `System.swift`,
`Profile.swift`, `Engine.swift`, `Prefs.swift` and `Tweaks.swift`.

RemoveMacAI builds on [pared](https://github.com/4evy/pared) by 4evy, which
first mapped Apple's Unified Asset Framework reset operation, the model asset
sets and several of the settings keys. The asset-service interface
(`Sources/UAF` in RemoveMacAI) and the model catalog follow pared's work.

Both projects are distributed under the MIT License.
